    What the app does with them

    In the app these sliders blend four layers: drizzle drops, the real rain recording, a heavy hiss and a low rumble. On this page the same numbers move the equations’ settings instead (the arrows show where each one goes), so you can hear and see what each slider does.

    Tip for sharing: press “Build it automatically”, then record your screen. It plays each step for eight seconds before adding the next. Keyboard: space plays or stops, the right arrow adds a step, the left arrow removes one. The drop model follows Minnaert’s bubble resonance and van den Doel’s physically based liquid sounds. The finished “Rain in Tayf” is tuned to match the frequency balance of the Rain in the Tayf app.
