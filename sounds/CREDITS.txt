Real recordings used in Tayf
============================
All three are released under Creative Commons 0 (public domain): free to use, change and sell, no credit
required. Credit is given here anyway, with thanks.

river.m4a  - "Relaxing, Mountains, Rivers, Streams, Running Water" by INNORECORDS
             https://freesound.org/people/INNORECORDS/sounds/469009/
             Used in: Tayf page, Deep Relax preset.
             Cut 0:15-1:51, low cut 45 Hz, top softened, levelled.

fire.m4a   - "Aachen_Burning Fireplace Crackling Fire Sounds" by visionear
             https://freesound.org/people/visionear/sounds/501417/
             Used in: Ambient page, Fire.
             Cut 7:42-9:18, room rumble removed (high-pass 70 Hz), gentle compression so the pops
             don't jump out, top softened, levelled.

rain.m4a   - "Gentle Rain from Window" by YostPeter
             https://freesound.org/people/YostPeter/sounds/523405/
             Used in: Ambient page, Rain.
             Cut 0:37-2:13, distant traffic rumble removed (high-pass 250 Hz), hiss softened, levelled.

Each clip is 96 s: the app folds the last 6 s over the start so it loops without a seam.
The full downloads live in sounds/_source/ on the owner's PC (not in git).

thunder-normal.m4a, thunder-loud.m4a - two thunder strikes provided by the owner (2026-11-09).
             Used in: Mix page, Thunder (single strikes at random, never looped; the loud one only from
             Distance 70% up). Leading silence trimmed, peaks limited to -1 dBFS (the loud one was clipping),
             AAC 160 kbps. Originals in sounds/_source/.
